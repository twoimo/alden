# Source content lines in graph titles

A blank stored title could make the graph display a legacy export's `- 링크: URL` or reaction-counter header. The popup retained the original source, but the graph's visible title did not describe the content. An actual preselected lookup probe exposed four such sources; changing title ranking alone did not repair these display headings.

The existing fallback now skips an entire URL/link field and the exact observed `반응: 좋아요 … · 답글 … · 리포스트 …` header, then selects a literal body line under the existing Korean/English length preferences. A saved nonblank title still wins. Sentences that discuss links or numerical results remain eligible. Header-only material gets no invented title and keeps its existing identity fallback. The helper returns once the first qualifying long Korean line is known; short Korean/English fallback priority is preserved.

A frozen permitted-source projection covered 26,623 documents. Exactly 10,101 derived titles changed, all from recognized pointer/counter lines; 825 had no remaining title candidate. Every new nonempty title is a literal normalized body line. All other titles were identical. Four actual graph-node/popup pairs retained the exact popup response, including body, source ID/version/hash, target, original link and capture reference. The full graph still reports 26,623 nodes and 48,355 relations. The bounded 1,984-node overview keeps 1,906 representatives and replaces 78 after the display-sampling decision; underlying identities are retained.

Two controlled tests failed before the fix. Affected collection, retrieval, source-text, quality and affinity suites passed 208 tests. Saved titles, substantive URL/numeric sentences, distinct same-heading sources, immutable stored labels/bodies/blobs and version-bound detail reads are covered.

A hidden IAB used the production `unifiedGraph`, `KnowledgeHologram` and CSS on the paired actual 1,984-node projections and a four-source subset. All four screenshots were directly inspected. The full views showed six labels, the subsets four; overlap, legend occlusion and clipping counts were zero, source objects were unchanged and browser warnings/errors were zero. This harness omits optional affinity enrichment and does not establish native input, Retina, physical minimization, semantic community validity, FPS or GPU/battery performance. The initial harness used the default 120-node parser path; only the corrected production-union 1,984-node receipts support the full-view result. Owned QA tabs/server were closed and original research tabs retained.

Two alternating graph-page repetitions per method were approximately 1.6–1.8 seconds. These are scoped diagnostics, not p95 or a whole-app speedup claim. The new read-only probe initially assumed that an optimized detail-only response included graph nodes and that popup provenance headings equal derived graph titles; the corrected probe reads both supported boundaries and requires the entire popup response to remain identical. Initial failures are retained separately.

Source target 0.3.207 will be built, signed and installed through the normal delivery path, with installation/settings/source readback and CI recorded separately under `outputs/display-link-title-20261021/`.

The ranking candidate stays uninstalled. The added source-derived probes found no hit-at-five improvement on nine new cases (five for each method), while the original case remains baseline rank24 versus candidate rank1. A title-only minimum-eight preflight failed with six available samples; adding body-derived headings does not satisfy that gate. Independent human semantic/identity/temporal gold and the preferred external audit remain open. No new title model, merge, source rewrite or database/index policy is introduced. The full 18-item objective and ST01–13 remain active.
