# Retained addresses and article body evidence

Alden 0.3.113 uses `source-content-evidence-v5`. A retained body consisting only of HTTP(S) addresses is classified as `reference_only`, even when indexed flags are true or the retained bytes are verified. This establishes the shape of the saved text; it does not fetch the destination, establish its contents or judge whether the source is useful. Prose with links, quoted addresses and short authored text remain content candidates. Explicit metadata/unread/derived-summary exclusions retain precedence.

References keep their source IDs, current versions, original text, links, declared tags and structural relationships. They remain available through search and explicit source lookup. They are excluded from persistent content labels, body grounding preference, content-based similarity forces and identity confirmation. Existing vectors and sources are not rewritten. Retrieval labels them as link references with the destination body unverified. Literal reference-text repetitions are reported separately from body repetitions and do not establish source/identity equivalence.

Tag inspection can compare verified declarations on either substantive body records or references. Candidate member pages compute v5 qualification from the verified full projected body before truncation and display an unread-destination notice for references. The tag inspection scope is `permitted_current_body_records_retained_bytes`. Current consumers reject pre-v5 affinity/quality/confirmation data, while historical v4 proof fields remain readable with their original qualification.
